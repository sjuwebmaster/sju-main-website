About the Climate Clock

The Climate Clock is a tool designed to visualize the urgency of addressing climate change. It counts down the remaining time until the global temperature rise reaches 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.

About the World Emissions Clock

The World Emissions Clock tracks real-time global carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions, displaying the total amount emitted each second alongside historical data.
